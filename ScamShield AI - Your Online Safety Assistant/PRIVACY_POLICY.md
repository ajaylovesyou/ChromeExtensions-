# Privacy Policy for ScamShield AI

**Effective Date:** October 12, 2026  
**Developer:** ScamShield AI Team

ScamShield AI ("we", "our", or "the extension") is dedicated to protecting your online safety while strictly respecting and defending your personal privacy.

### 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
* **We do NOT collect, store, or transmit your personal data.**
* ScamShield AI runs entirely locally inside your browser.
* We do not collect names, email addresses, passwords, credit cards, browsing history, or physical locations.

### 2. How Permissions Are Used
* **`activeTab` & `<all_urls>`:** Used exclusively on your local computer to inspect the address bar and web page elements for deceptive links, brand impersonation (lookalikes), and fraudulent countdown timers.
* **`storage`:** Used solely to save your local preferences (such as enabling/disabling link inspection) and your local scan history on your own device (`chrome.storage.local`). This history never leaves your computer.
* **`contextMenus`:** Enables the right-click shortcut so you can voluntarily scan a specific link or suspicious text snippet.

### 3. Third-Party Sharing
* We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your data to any advertising brokers or third parties.
* If you optionally configure external API keys (such as Google Safe Browsing), only the specific URL requested is sent for threat lookup over encrypted HTTPS.

### 4. Children's Privacy
ScamShield AI does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

### 5. Contact & Support
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via GitHub Issues or support contact.
